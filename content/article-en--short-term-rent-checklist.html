🏡 How to prepare a property for short-term rental: a checklist from the experts

The difference between an average Airbnb and an exceptional Airbnb isn't just location. It's the details that make the difference. This guide will help you turn an ordinary apartment into a popular and profitable property.

💡 Why is it worth investing in quality equipment?

The modern guest expects more than just a bed and a bathroom. Quality amenities will ensure:

✅ Higher ratings and better reviews
✅ Option to charge premium prices
✅ Higher occupancy and longer stays
✅ Fewer complaints and breakdowns

Fact: A well-prepared property can increase occupancy by up to 40 %.

🛠️ Furnishing philosophy: 'Cheap is expensive'

Principle 1: Avoid the cheapest furniture from sales. Instead, invest in durable and practical solutions.

Recommended Strategy:

  • 🛋 Modern and timeless design
  • 💶 Medium price category with long service life
  • 🧼 Easy maintenance and cleaning
  • 🛠️ Warranties and replacement in case of damage

✅ Complete checklist by room

🛏 Bedroom - The basis of comfort

  • ✅ Quality mattress (5-10 years lifetime)
  • ✅ Bed with fixed frame
  • ✅ Min. 3 sets of bed linen
  • ✅ Pillows of different hardness
  • ✅ Blackout curtains for quality sleep
  • ✅ Bedside tables and lamps
  • ✅ Large mirror, laundry basket, alarm clock

🍳 Kitchen - The heart of every stay

  • ✅ Refrigerator with freezer
  • ✅ Stove/cooktop
  • ✅ Microwave, kettle, coffee maker
  • ✅ Dishes for 6-8 persons
  • ✅ Complete cutlery sets
  • ✅ Pans, pots, plates, knives
  • ✅ Cleaning products, wipes, gloves

🛁 Bathroom - Relaxation and hygiene

  • ✅ Shower with good pressure
  • ✅ Mirror and hangers
  • ✅ 3 sets of towels (small/medium/large)
  • ✅ Bath mat, laundry basket
  • ✅ Quality shampoo, shower gel, soap
  • ✅ Hairdryer, toilet paper

🛋 Living room - Common area

  • ✅ Comfortable sofa + armchair
  • ✅ Smart TV (Netflix, YouTube)
  • ✅ Fast Wifi router
  • ✅ Coffee table, cushions, shelves
  • ✅ Board games, books, decorations

🏠 General equipment

  • 🔒 Fire alarm, fire extinguisher, first aid kit
  • 🌬 Air conditioning, fans, humidifier
  • 🧹 Mop, bucket, vacuum cleaner, dryer
  • 👚 Iron + ironing board

🎯 Investment priorities

1. Necessities (70 %):

  • Mattress, appliances, basic toiletries, wifi

2. Comfort (20 %):

  • Air conditioning, curtains, decorations, extra seating

3. Luxus (10 %):

  • Premium cosmetics, quality towels, coffee machine

❌ The most common mistakes

  1. Generic look without character
  2. Cheapest equipment - breaks down quickly
  3. Poor lighting
  4. Lack of storage space
  5. Uncomfortable beds
  6. Few drawers
  7. Weak air conditioning

🧬 How to create the character of the property?

✔ Choose colour palette (1 dominant colour)
✔ Select Style - industrial, Scandinavian, modern
✔ Add local elements - Slovak ceramics, photography
✔ Use interesting lighting
✔ Pay attention to consistency of style

🛍 Recommended suppliers and brands

Look for partners who understand the specifics of Airbnb:

  • Fast delivery and exchange option
  • Service and warranty
  • Long-term cooperation
  • Experience with rentals

🎨 Style & Design: what works?

  • 🎨 Neutral colours + 1 bold element
  • 🛠 Quality and practical materials
  • 📸 Good photogenic detail for gallery
  • 🪵 Brick, metal, wood - they always impress
  • 🎭 Personalization: slovak elements, wine, books

📈 ROI - Return on Investment

🧾 Final checklist before the guest

  • ✅ Functional equipment (appliances, wifi, air conditioning)
  • ✅ Cleaned professionally
  • ✅ Adequate supplies (toilet paper, soap, coffee)
  • ✅ Prepared information for guests
  • ✅ Visual detail for photos
  • ✅ Test stay and correction of deficiencies

🏁 Conclusion: apartment with character = higher profit

Facilities for Airbnb's an investment, not an expense. Thoughtful details, comfort and unique style will ensure your guests leave satisfied and happy to return - or recommend your apartment to others.

Tip: The best feedback is that which guests share with their friends.
