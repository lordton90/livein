5-bedroom urban residence at a prestigious address

For sale

Apartment Moyzesova, Bratislava-Staré Mesto, district Bratislava I, Slovakia

Description

In one of the most representative addresses of Bratislava's Old Town, a space has been created that combines historical value with modern comfort.

The five-room apartment on Moyzesova Street represents an exceptional combination of architecture, space and atmosphere that is rarely seen today.

Architecture with respect for the past The apartment is located in an Art Nouveau house from 1903 and has undergone a complete reconstruction under the guidance of architect Ilya Skocek.

Each intervention was made with an emphasis on preserving authenticity while creating a timeless interior.

The original wooden parquet floors, high ceilings and craftsmanship details remain a dominant feature of the space, while modern elements naturally complement the overall character of the living space.

The total area of 155 m² provides generous space without compromise.

Layout designed for life The interior is divided into day and night zones with an emphasis on flow and functionality.

The living area consists of an open composition of kitchen, dining room and living room with fireplace.

This space is both representative and intimate at the same time.

The master bedroom has its own bathroom and walk-in closet, creating a private zone separate from the rest of the apartment.

The other three bedrooms offer flexibility of use - as children's rooms, study or guest rooms.

Of course, there is a second bathroom, a separate toilet and a utility room.

Comfort without visible compromise The apartment is sold partially furnished, while the furnishings respect the architecture of the space and its character.

The technical standard includes its own gas boiler, preparation for air conditioning and security system.

The apartment comes with a cellar, a loft space and a front garden in the courtyard, which provides privacy and silence in the heart of the city.

Location as value Moyzesova Street is one of the most sought-after addresses in Bratislava.

The immediate proximity of the Presidential Palace, Grassalkovich Garden or Medica Garden creates a unique urban environment with plenty of greenery.

Within walking distance there are complete amenities, cultural institutions and quality gastronomy.

The location also offers excellent accessibility within the city.

Parking The apartment has a parking space in the yard.

Price: 990.000 EUR A property that offers not just housing, but long term value, character and distinction.
