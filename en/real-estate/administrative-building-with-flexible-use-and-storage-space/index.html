Office building with flexible use and warehouse space

For sale

Mramorová, Bratislava-Podunajské Biskupice, district Bratislava II, Slovakia

Description

For sale office building with extensive warehouse space and private parking on Mramorova street in Bratislava - Podunajské Biskupice.

The property represents an ideal solution for companies looking for a combination of administration, storage and excellent accessibility within the city.

A well thought out space for business.

The building consists of two floors with the possibility of easy division into two separate functional units.

This flexibility allows use for one larger company or as an investment opportunity with multi-tenant lease.

The interior is modern and presentable, ready for immediate use without the need for major alterations.

The layout includes: office space of various sizes several social facilities kitchenette for staff archive in the underground part functional facilities and storage capacities The exterior offers an enclosed yard with plenty of room for both handling and parking.

The dominant feature is the oversized garage which is ideal for storage, servicing or logistical use.

From the courtyard there is also access to a separate part of the building with two offices and its own sanitary facilities, which allows for separate operation or rental.

Parking without restrictions The property has a sufficient number of parking spaces directly in front of the building and within the enclosed courtyard, which is a significant advantage in this location.
