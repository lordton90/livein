Fully renovated 3-room apartment with a loggia

For sale

Apartment Jelšová, Bratislava - Nové Mesto

Description

We are offering for sale a spacious 3-room apartment with an area of 56 m² that has been completely renovated (2026), located on the 9th floor of a brick apartment building in a quiet and sought-after neighborhood of Bratislava’s Kramáre district, on Jelšová Street.

LAYOUT The apartment consists of three separate rooms, a separate kitchen, a bathroom, and a separate toilet.

It includes a 4 m² southwestern-facing loggia and a 1.5 m² basement storage room.

CONDITION AND RENOVATION The apartment underwent a complete renovation in 2026 and is ready for immediate move-in.

AMENITIES INCLUDED IN THE PRICE Included in the price of the apartment are a new kitchen set with appliances (refrigerator, dishwasher, electric oven, range hood), a dining table with chairs, a sofa set, and two beds.
