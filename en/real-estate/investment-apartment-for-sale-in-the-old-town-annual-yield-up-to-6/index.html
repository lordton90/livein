Investment apartment for sale in Old Town - annual yield up to 6 %

Sold

Description

We offer for sale a fully furnished 3-room apartment after reconstruction in an exceptional location on Špitálská street in the historical centre of Bratislava, just a few minutes walk from the pedestrian zone.

The apartment is located on the 2nd floor of a brick apartment building with elevator and has an area of 84 m².

Ideal investment apartment in the Old Town - with an annual yield of up to 6 % and a passive income of around 24 000 €.

The layout consists of a living room connected to the kitchen, two bedrooms, hall, bathroom, separate toilet, chambers and loggia.

One of the bedrooms is oriented to the street, the other to a quiet courtyard.

The entrance to the apartment leads through the pavilion, while it is the last apartment on the pavilion, which guarantees more privacy.

The apartment has undergone a quality renovation in 2019 and is sold fully furnished including appliances.

It has a pleasant atmosphere also thanks to the high ceilings that create an airy and elegant space.

It is suitable as a comfortable urban living, but also as an investment.

In the past it was successfully used for short-term rentals (Airbnb, Booking) under the management of our real estate company.

Detailed historical data on yields and occupancy is available.

Thanks to its location in the heart of the Old Town, it offers all the advantages of city life.

Within walking distance there are shops, cafes, restaurants, cultural institutions and public transport stops.
